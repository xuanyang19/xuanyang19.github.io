Xuan (Lily) Yang 杨萱

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Duke University, advised by Jian Pei, with expected graduation in 2027.

I study data-centric AI: how to value and curate data, and how to use it more effectively in learning and reasoning. My work spans data valuation, LLM reasoning, and agent evaluation. I am currently exploring data curation for LLMs and agent memory.

Previously, I received my bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Zhejiang University, where I worked with Yang Yang. I have also worked at Google, TikTok, and Alibaba.

Our work on DropMessage received an AAAI 2023 Distinguished Paper Award.
